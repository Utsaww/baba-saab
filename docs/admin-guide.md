# Baba Saab Digital Invitations — Staff Guide

This guide grows as new parts of the invitation system go live. Everything described here works today.

## What you can do right now

| Area | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Your starting page after signing in. |
| **Invitations** | Create invitations for customers, fill in their details and continue saved drafts. |
| **Templates** | Browse the 4 invitation designs with sample details, try their colour palettes and languages, and show them to customers. |
| **Music** | The shared Music Library: songs any invitation can use as background music. |
| **Staff** | *Owner only.* Invite people to the admin area and remove them. |
| **Help** | This guide. |

Guests can RSVP on WhatsApp: their reply opens WhatsApp to the family's number. Turn it on in **Editor step 7: Settings**.

## Signing in

The admin area is private. Everyone who uses it has their own account, which the owner creates.

**Your first sign-in**

1. Look for an email titled **Your Baba Saab Invitations staff account**. Check your spam folder if it isn't in your inbox.
2. Open the sign-in link in the email.
3. Enter your email address and the **temporary password** from the email, then click **Sign in**.
4. Choose your own password and enter it twice. It needs at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol.

The temporary password works for 7 days. If it has expired, ask the owner to send your invitation again.

**Forgot your password?** On the sign-in page click **Forgot your password?**, enter your email, and type in the code you're emailed along with a new password.

**Signing out.** Click **Sign out** at the bottom of the left menu. Always sign out on a shared computer.

**"No access" message.** Your account isn't set up as staff. Ask the owner to invite you, then sign in with the email address they used.

## The dashboard

After signing in you land on the **Dashboard**. It links to everything you can use. Your invitations and upcoming events will appear here as those features arrive. RSVPs go to the family's WhatsApp, not to Baba Saab, so they don't appear here.

The yellow **New here?** banner links to this guide. Click **Dismiss** to hide it on this computer.

## Managing staff (owner only)

Only the owner sees **Staff** in the left menu.

**Inviting someone**

1. Open **Staff**.
2. Under **Invite a staff member**, enter their name and email address, then click **Send invitation**.
3. They receive an email with a sign-in link and a temporary password. Their row shows **Invited · hasn't signed in yet** until they sign in for the first time.

**They didn't get the email, or it expired?** Ask them to check spam first. Then click **Send invite again** on their row. This sends a fresh temporary password, valid for another 7 days.

**Removing someone**

1. Click **Remove** on their row.
2. Click **Yes, remove** to confirm.

They can't sign in again. If they're signed in at that moment, their session ends within the hour. You can't remove yourself or the owner account.

## Invitations

Open **Invitations** in the left menu to see every invitation, with the most recently edited at the top. Each row shows the couple's names, the design, the wedding date, its status and who edited it last.

- **Search:** type part of a name (English or Hindi) or a phone number.
- **Status:** choose a status to see only those invitations. New invitations are **Draft** until they are published. After that, a badge shows **Live** (guests see the latest version) or **Changes not live** (you have edits guests can't see yet). Live invitations have an **Open** link that shows the guest page. **Offline** and **Deleted** invitations are covered in the sections below.
- **All** hides deleted invitations. Choose **Deleted** to find them.
- Click a row to open it in the editor.
- **Quick buttons on each row**, next to **Open**, save a trip into the editor. Each one asks you to confirm first (Restore doesn't), and the row updates straight away:
  - **Take offline** (the power icon) appears on live invitations. Guests then see "no longer available". You can put it back online for 30 days.
  - **Delete** (the bin icon) appears on every invitation that isn't already deleted. It moves to Deleted and is erased after 30 days unless restored.
  - **Restore** appears on deleted invitations and brings them back. If a live invitation's **Take offline on** date has already passed, the row says **Pick a date after today** with a link to open the invitation and choose a new date.
  - If something goes wrong, a red message appears on that row. **This invitation changed — reload the page** means someone else edited it, so reload and try again.
  - **Put back online** and **Delete permanently now** are only in the editor.

**Notices above the list** appear only when they apply, and clicking one filters the list:

- **N invitations go offline in the next 7 days** shows the live invitations that will soon go offline by themselves.
- **N will be erased in the next 24 hours** shows the offline or deleted invitations about to be permanently erased.

A deleted invitation's badge also says when it will be erased, for example **Deleted · erased on 4 November 2026**. It reads **erasing…** once that moment has passed and the daily job hasn't reached it yet.

## Creating an invitation

1. Open **Invitations** and click **New invitation**.
2. Pick a **design**. The phone preview next to (or below) the options shows it with sample details.
3. Pick a **colour palette** and the **language**: English, हिंदी, or **Both** (each line in English with Hindi underneath).
4. Click **Create invitation**. The editor opens on step 2, ready for the couple's details.

You can change the design, colours and language later without losing anything you've typed.

## Editing an invitation

The editor has three parts: the **steps** on the left, the **form** in the middle and a **live phone preview** on the right (on a phone, tap **Preview**).

- Do the steps in any order. A **✓** appears next to each step that has details filled in.
- **Saving is automatic.** About 3 seconds after you stop typing, the top right says **Saving…** and then **Saved** with the time.
- **"Couldn't save" banner:** your changes are still on the screen. Check the internet connection and click **Retry**. Don't close the page until it says **Saved**.
- **"Updated by someone else" banner:** another staff member saved this invitation while you had it open. Click **Reload** to see their version, then make your changes again. This stops two people overwriting each other.
- If you click a link or sign out while a save is still pending, your changes are saved first. If you close or reload the tab before it says **Saved**, the browser warns you — stay on the page until it says **Saved**. If your latest changes couldn't be saved, the page warns you before you leave.
- Click **? Help for this step** to jump to that step's section of this guide.

## Editor step 1: Template & palette

Change the design, colour palette or language at any time. Nothing you've typed is lost. If you switch from **Both** to English, the Hindi text is kept, and it comes back if you switch to **Both** again.

## Editor step 2: Couple & families

- **Bride and groom:** names, and the parents line, for example "D/o Smt. Sunita & Shri Rajesh Sharma".
- **Blessing at the top:** the symbol (Shri Ganesh, Om or none) and a shloka. Pick one of the ready-made shlokas, or choose **Our own text** and type the family's own.
- **Families:** add each family's name for the closing message. **Closing line** is the sign-off, for example "With love and blessings from the Sharma and Mehra families".
- **Family contact phone:** shown to guests in the closing message at the end of the invitation. Search on the Invitations page also finds invitations by this number. **If guests reply on WhatsApp (step 7), this must be the family's WhatsApp number**, because every RSVP is sent there. The invitation can't be published without it while RSVPs are on.

For a **Both** invitation, each field has an English box and a Hindi box.

## Editor step 3: Date, venue & travel

- **Main wedding date:** drives the countdown and the save-the-date.
- **Venue name and address.**
- **Google Maps link:** open the venue in Google Maps, tap **Share**, copy the link and paste it. It must start with `https://`.
- **Travel notes:** nearest airport and railway station, weather tips. Put one point per line.

## Editor step 4: Events

Add each function guests are invited to. The quick buttons (**+ Haldi**, **+ Mehendi**, **+ Sangeet**, **+ Vivah**, **+ Reception**) fill in the name in English and Hindi. Use **+ Other event** for anything else. Give every event a date and time. Add a different venue, dress code or description only where it applies. Use **Move up** and **Move down** to change the order.

## Editor step 5: Photos, film & music

**Photos you can use:** JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP and iPhone photos (HEIC). Pixel phone photos ("PXL_…") are ordinary JPGs. iPhone photos are converted automatically. Every photo is resized and compressed so the invitation loads quickly on phones. Photos sent to you on WhatsApp work too.

- **Cover photo:** the big photo at the top of the invitation. Click **Upload**, pick a photo, and wait for the bar to finish. Use **Replace** or **Remove** to change it.
- **Couple photos (optional):** one for the bride and one for the groom, shown in "The couple" section.
- **Photo gallery:** click **Add photos** and pick up to 12 at once. Each photo can have a caption. Use the arrows to change the order and **Remove** to take one out. When the gallery holds 12 photos it's full.
- **Invitation film:** paste the film's **YouTube link**. The film can be "unlisted" on YouTube.
- **Background music:** choose **No music**, a song **From the Music Library**, or **Upload a song for this invitation** (MP3 up to 10 MB). Press **▶ Play** to listen first. Only upload music you have the rights to use.

If an upload fails, click **Retry** next to it, or **Dismiss** to drop it. The rest of the invitation isn't affected. Don't close the page while a photo is still uploading.

Removing a cover or couple photo asks you to confirm first, because it can't be undone.

## Editor step 6: Sections

Choose which parts of the invitation guests see, and in what order. Untick **Show** to hide a section, and use the arrows to move it. The cover always comes first. A section with nothing filled in is hidden automatically, so you only need to hide sections you've filled in but don't want shown.

## Editor step 7: Settings

- **Take offline on** appears here once the invitation is live. It is the date the invitation goes offline by itself. See **After the wedding** below.
- **Guests reply on WhatsApp** adds an RSVP form to the invitation. When a guest sends it, WhatsApp opens with their reply already typed, addressed to the family contact number from step 2. Nothing is stored by Baba Saab. When it's on, you can also set:
  - **Last day to reply** (optional). After this day the form closes and shows the family's phone number instead.
  - **Ask how many people are coming** (on unless you untick it).
  - **Ask about meal preference** (vegetarian, non-vegetarian or Jain).
- **Show "Created by Baba Saab Events"** adds a small credit at the bottom. Untick it if the customer prefers.

See **RSVPs on WhatsApp** below for what guests see.

## Editor step 8: Review

This step lists everything still needed before the invitation can be published, such as a missing date or an event without a time. Click **Go to step N** to fix each one. If guests reply on WhatsApp, it also asks for the family's WhatsApp number (step 2). When the list is clear it says **Everything needed is filled in.** The rest of the step is where you choose the link name, share the customer preview link and publish. Each part is explained below.

### Choosing the link name

The **Link name** is the last part of the invitation's web address, for example `/invitation/riya-and-arjun`. A name is suggested from the couple's names, and saved for you when possible. To change it:

1. Type a new name. Use lowercase letters, numbers and dashes only (capitals are changed to lowercase for you).
2. Wait a moment for **✓ available**. If the name is taken or not allowed, a red message says why.
3. Click **Save link name**.

Choose the name before sharing any link, because it is **locked once the invitation is published**. Links already sent to guests keep working.

### Preview links for customers

As soon as the link name is saved, **Customer preview link** appears. Use it to show the customer their invitation before it goes live.

- **Copy** copies the link. **Send on WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with a ready message.
- The preview always shows the **latest saved draft**, with a "Preview — not published yet" banner at the top. Guests never see the banner on the live link.
- If the link went to the wrong person, click **New preview link** and confirm. The old link stops working ("This invitation is no longer available") and you get a fresh one.

### Publishing

Click **Publish** when the checklist is clear. The button is greyed out while anything is still needed, while photos or music are still uploading ("Wait for uploads to finish"), or until a link name is saved. A confirmation asks you to check, because the link name can't be changed afterwards.

After publishing you'll see:

- **Live link for guests:** the link to send. **Copy** and **Send on WhatsApp** work as above.
- **Download QR code:** see QR codes below.
- **Open:** shows the live invitation in a new tab.

### Making changes after publishing

Edits keep saving automatically, but they are private. Guests keep seeing the version that was published until you update it.

- A yellow note says **You have changes guests can't see yet**, with an **Update live invitation** button. It also appears at the top of the other steps. Click it when you're ready, and guests see the change within seconds.
- Before updating, the customer can check the changes on their **preview link**, which already shows your latest edits.
- In the invitations list, the badge reads **Changes not live** until you update.

### WhatsApp preview cards

When the live link is pasted into WhatsApp, it shows a card with the cover photo, the couple's names and the wedding date. Preview links show a plain generic card, so nothing is given away early. WhatsApp keeps cards for a while, so people who already received the link may see an older card after you change the photo, names or date. New recipients see the latest.

### QR codes

After publishing, click **Download QR code**. It saves a file named like `riya-and-arjun-qr.png` that points to the live link. It prints sharply at normal invitation and standee sizes. Always scan it with a phone before sending it to print.

### RSVPs on WhatsApp

When **Guests reply on WhatsApp** is on in step 7, the invitation has an RSVP form: name, phone, whether they're coming, how many (if asked), which functions, meal (if asked) and a message.

- **Sending:** the guest taps **Send RSVP on WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens with their reply typed out, addressed to the family's number. The guest just presses send in WhatsApp. Replies arrive in the family's WhatsApp, in the invitation's language (English for a **Both** invitation).
- **If WhatsApp doesn't open** (for example on a computer without WhatsApp), the reply is shown with **Copy message** and **Open WhatsApp** buttons, and the family's number to send it to.
- **Nothing is stored by Baba Saab.** The guest's phone remembers their last reply, so if they come back the form is already filled in and they can change it and send again.
- **After the last day to reply**, the form is replaced by a note with the family's phone number for changes.
- **Previews:** the form works the same in the customer preview and the editor, with a note that replies go to the family's WhatsApp. Test it with your own phone. If the family's number is missing, previews say so, and the live invitation hides the form.

## After the wedding

Every live invitation goes offline by itself some time after the wedding, so old links don't stay up forever.

- **Default:** 7 days after the last event, in India time. It is set when you publish. If you publish after that day has already passed, it is set to tomorrow instead, so the invitation doesn't go offline the same night.
- **Invitations published before this feature:** the daily job gives each one a date the first night it runs: 7 days after its last event, and never earlier than the next day. Check step 7 if a customer needs a different date.
- **Changing it:** open the invitation's step 7 (**Settings**), change **Take offline on** and click **Save date**. Today or any later date is allowed; today means it goes offline at the next daily run. If it falls before the last event, a note says so, but you can still save it.
- The daily job runs around 3 am India time and takes offline everything whose date has arrived.
- The invitations list warns you when invitations are due to go offline in the next 7 days.

## Taking an invitation offline and putting it back

**Take it offline now:** open the live invitation, click the **⋯** menu at the top right and choose **Take offline now**. Confirm. Guests who open the link see "This invitation is no longer available". The same happens when the **Take offline on** date arrives.

An offline invitation shows a grey bar saying **Offline since [date] — will be erased on [date]**. Its badge in the list is **Offline**.

**Put it back online:** click **Put back online** on that bar, choose a new **Take offline on** date (it must be after today) and click **Put online**. The same link works again straight away. This only works until the invitation is erased, 30 days after it went offline.

## Deleting and restoring

**Deleting:** open the **⋯** menu on any invitation that isn't already deleted and choose **Delete**. Confirm. The invitation disappears from **All** and appears under **Deleted**. If it was live, its link stops working.

A deleted invitation is **read-only**. You can look at it but not change it. Its grey bar says **Deleted — will be erased on [date]**.

**Restoring:** click **Restore** on the bar. The invitation comes back as it was: a draft is a draft again, and a live one is live again. If the invitation was live and its **Take offline on** date is today, has already passed, or was never set, you are asked for a new date first. Pick a date after today and click **Restore with this date**.

## Permanent erase

Offline and deleted invitations are erased for good **30 days** after they went offline or were deleted. The daily job (around 3 am India time) does this. Erasing can't be undone.

**What is erased:** the invitation, its photos, its film link, music uploaded for this invitation and its link name, which can then be used again.

**What is kept:** songs in the Music Library, even if the invitation used them.

**Erasing sooner:** on a deleted invitation click **Delete permanently now**. Type the invitation's link name (or the word `delete` if it has none) exactly as shown, then click **Erase now**.

## Music Library

Open **Music** in the left menu to see the songs every invitation can use.

**Adding a song:** click **Choose MP3** and pick an MP3 file (up to 10 MB). The name fills in from the file name; change it if you like. Then click **Upload song**. Only upload music you have the rights to use.

**Listening:** press **▶ Play** next to a song.

**Renaming:** click **Rename**, type the new name and click **Save name**.

**Deleting:** click **Delete**. A song that any invitation uses can't be deleted. The page shows which invitations use it, so you can change their music first.

## Browsing templates

1. Open **Templates** in the left menu.
2. Each card shows a live phone preview of one design with sample details, its name, a short description and its colour palettes (the small circles).
3. Click **Preview** on a card to open that design.

## Previewing a template with a customer

On a template's preview page:

- **Palette** — tap a palette name to recolour the design. Every template has 3 palettes.
- **Language** — choose **English**, **हिंदी** or **Both**. "Both" shows each line in English with Hindi underneath.
- **Open full screen ↗** — opens the design in a new tab. Use this to show it on a laptop or to send the page to your own phone.
- The opening animation replays every time you change a setting.

> The names, dates and photos are sample details. A customer's own details are added when invitations can be created.

## The four designs

| Template | Look | How guests open it |
|---|---|---|
| **Royal Rajasthani** | Maroon and gold, palace-arch frame | Scratch a gold card to reveal the wedding date (or tap "or tap here") |
| **Floral Pastel** | Soft watercolour florals | Scroll or swipe up (or tap) |
| **Temple Classic** | Temple motifs and kolam borders | Tap the sealed envelope |
| **Modern Minimal** | Monogram cover, clean typography | Tap to reveal |

Every design includes the same sections. Ones without details are hidden automatically:

Invocation (Shri Ganesh / Om and a shloka) · The couple and their parents · Save the date · Live countdown · Venue with Google Maps · Travel notes · Schedule of functions with "Add to calendar" · Photo gallery · Invitation film · Closing message with family names and a WhatsApp share button

## Choosing a template with a customer — tips

- **Ask about the mood first.** Grand and traditional suits Royal Rajasthani or Temple Classic. Soft and romantic suits Floral Pastel. A reception or destination wedding often suits Modern Minimal.
- **Show it on a phone.** Nearly every guest will open the invitation on a phone, so use **Open full screen ↗** on your own phone.
- **Settle the language early.** If elders in the family prefer Hindi, choose **Both**.

## Details to collect from the customer

Gather these before invitations can be created, so the next step is quick:

- Bride's and groom's names (in Hindi too, if they want both languages)
- Parents' names for "D/o…" and "S/o…" lines
- Family names for the closing message, and a contact phone number on WhatsApp (guests' RSVPs are sent there)
- Main wedding date, venue name, full address and a Google Maps link
- Every function: name, date, time, venue (if different), dress code
- Travel notes: nearest airport and railway station, weather tips
- A cover photo and up to about 12 gallery photos
- The invitation film: a YouTube link, or a video file under 100 MB
- Background music: a song from our Music Library, or their own MP3 (under 10 MB) — only music they have the rights to use
- Which shloka to show: Ganesh Vandana (वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय…), Shri Ganeshaya Namah, Mangal Shloka, Shubh Vivah, Om, or their own text
